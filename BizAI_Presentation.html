BizAI

AI-Powered Local Commerce Platform

Connecting neighbourhood shops with customers through intelligent insights, seamless ordering, and real-time analytics — powered by Groq AI.

🛒 Two-Sided Marketplace 🤖 Groq AI Integration 📊 Real-Time Analytics 📱 Mobile-First ☁️ Cloud Deployed
⚠️ The Problem

Local shops are flying blind
in a digital world

Over 60 million kirana and local shops in India have no access to business intelligence, inventory management, or customer insights.

📉

No Data Insights

Shop owners don't know which products sell best, peak hours, or revenue trends — all tracked in notebooks.

📦

Manual Inventory

Stock tracking is done manually, leading to over-stocking, under-stocking, and missed sales opportunities.

👥

No Customer Connection

Customers have no way to browse inventory, place orders, or track their orders from local shops online.

🤖

Zero AI Access

Enterprise AI tools are expensive and complex. Local shop owners have no access to actionable AI-driven advice.

✅ Our Solution

One platform. Two sides. Infinite value.

🏪 For Shop Owners (Sellers)

📊

AI Business Dashboard

Real-time revenue, sales trends, and 7-day AI forecasts powered by Groq Llama 3.

🤖

AI Growth Advisor

Chat with an AI that knows your business data and gives personalised growth advice.

📦

Inventory Management

Track stock levels, set low-stock alerts, and manage your product catalogue.

🛒

Order Management

Receive, confirm, and manage customer orders in real-time with status updates.

📱 For Customers (Consumers)

🗺️

Discover Local Shops

Browse all nearby shops by category — Kirana, Food, Electronics, and more.

🛍️

Browse & Cart

View live inventory, search products, and add items to cart before visiting.

📋

Place Pickup Orders

Place orders online and pay in cash when you arrive at the shop.

📍

Track Order Status

Get real-time updates — Pending → Confirmed → Ready → Completed.

🤖 AI Intelligence Core

Groq AI makes every shop
smarter & more profitable

📈

Revenue Forecasting

AI analyses 7-day sales patterns with weekday/weekend variance to predict next week's revenue with confidence scores.

💡

Business Insights

Groq Llama 3 generates 5 personalised insights from your actual sales data — top products, growth opportunities, slow movers.

🏥

Business Health Score

A 0–100 score (Thriving / Stable / Growing / Struggling) based on revenue growth, customer retention, and transaction volume.

💬

AI Chat Advisor

Context-aware business chat — the AI knows your total revenue, recent sales, and customers to give tailored advice.

🎯

Growth Planning

AI-generated 30-60-90 day growth plan with specific, actionable steps based on your business stage and data.

⚡

Smart Fallback

Rule-based fallback system ensures insights always work — even if Groq API is unavailable, the shop owner gets useful data.
